02 — THE APPROACH

Connections you
can understand.

A recommendation is more useful when you know why it appeared. Hunter combines your library, provider metadata and your feedback to give discovery some context.

  1. 01

    Your Plex library

    The collection you have built is the starting point.

  2. 02

    Metadata enrichment

    Film, television and music context adds connections.

  3. 03

    Discovery & recommendations

    Understandable reasons help you choose what to explore.

  4. 04

    Your feedback

    You decide what interests you and what to pass over.

Local-first. Personally controlled.

Hunter runs privately, with external services providing metadata. Discovery reads your media library; any supported playlist changes are explicitly controlled.

Discovery, on your terms.

Your feedback stays under your control. Hunter helps you find possibilities, with no automatic media downloads or acquisitions.

03 — AN ONGOING PERSONAL PROJECT

Built for everyday curiosity.

Hunter is an actively used personal software project, running in a private deployment. This page shares the idea behind it; the application is not currently offered as a public download or hosted service.
